✈ From Frankfurt Airport (FRA)

FRA is the nearest major airport. By car it's about 1 hour (≈ 65 km) via Wiesbaden and the B42 along the Rhine. By train, take the RB10 (usually changing at Frankfurt Hbf or Wiesbaden) to Rüdesheim, roughly 1–1.5 h.

🚆 RB10 train

The RB10 regional train runs along the Rhine from Frankfurt (≈ 1 h) and Wiesbaden (≈ 30 min) to Rüdesheim (Rhein) station, a 4-minute walk from the villa. This is the easiest way to arrive on the wedding day if you're staying in Wiesbaden or Mainz. Times and tickets: bahn.de.

⛴ Scenic Friday arrival by boat

Arrive the romantic way on Friday 23 July: KD Köln-Düsseldorfer boats sail up the Rhine from Mainz (dep. 09:15) and Wiesbaden-Biebrich (dep. 09:30) to Rüdesheim, arriving 11:00. Fares are about €28 single. There's one sailing a day and it doesn't run on Mondays, so it's a day-before treat, not a wedding-day plan. Times are from the 2026 timetable; 2027 TBC. k-d.com

🚗 Bingen car ferry

Staying in Bingen? The Bingen–Rüdesheim car ferry runs daily, continuously from about 05:30 until about midnight in summer (2026 times). It costs about €2.70 on foot or €5.90 per car, and it isn't affected by low water. The small passenger ferry runs only during the day. Timetable

Rüdesheim am Rhein

Hotels, guesthouses and holiday apartments within walking distance of the venue and the riverfront. The easiest option for the evening.

Bingen am Rhein

Across the river. The car ferry runs until about midnight in summer, and there are left-bank trains to Mainz and Koblenz from Bingen Hbf.

Rheingau villages

Geisenheim, Oestrich-Winkel and Eltville are wine villages a short drive or train ride upriver.

Wiesbaden / Mainz

Cities about 35–45 minutes away with lots of hotels, good for combining the trip with sightseeing. You'll need transport to and from the venue.

Travel Tips

Entry & documents

Germany is in the Schengen Area. Finnish/EU citizens just need an ID card or passport. US citizens can usually visit visa-free for up to 90 days. Check whether the new EU ETIAS travel authorisation applies to your trip, and make sure your passport is valid for at least 3 months after you leave.

Money

The currency is the euro (€). Cards are widely accepted, but small cafés, wine stands and taxis may want cash. Keep some euros on hand.

Weather

Late July is usually warm, typically 20–30 °C (68–86 °F), with long evenings. Pack sunscreen, and a light layer for the night on the river.

Power

Type C/F plugs, 230 V. US guests need an adapter, and check that your devices support 230 V. Finnish plugs work as-is.

Language

German is spoken, and English is widely understood in Rüdesheim's tourist areas.

Things to do

  • Take the cable car over the vineyards to the Niederwald Monument. (The Assmannshausen chairlift is currently out of service.)
  • Stroll the Drosselgasse and sample Riesling at the Weinprobierstand.
  • Visit the Brömserburg wine museum next to the villa, or Siegfried's Mechanical Music Cabinet.
  • Take a Rhine cruise past the Mäuseturm, Burg Rheinstein and the Loreley.
  • Day trips: the Abbey of St. Hildegard and Kloster Eberbach.
